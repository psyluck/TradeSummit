Everything the platform is

Designed for settlement, engineered for speed.

Every tick, order, and transfer is a verifiable record — not a promise.

Atomic settlement

Orders execute on-chain on Hyperliquid's L1 order book, so trade and settlement happen as one operation — no counterparty drift.

USDC-native rails

Settle in USDC for a stable unit of account, with fiat on/off ramps handled through sanctioned stablecoin liquidity.

Verifiable risk

Type-safe margin accounting keeps a hard 10x leverage cap on every position — no black-box risk engine.

Provable audit trail

Every state change is written to an immutable ledger with cryptographic linkage — auditable end to end, verifiable by anyone.

Self-custody posture

Keys remain under your control. The platform coordinates — it never holds what you own.

Resilient by design

Built on the BEAM's fault-tolerant runtime with supervised services, so redeploys and failures don't cost you a trade.

How it works

From order to record in three steps.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Bring liquidity from USDC pools and link your wallet or custody solution. Identity verified once, at the edge.

  2. 02

    Match

    Hyperliquid's CLOB matches orders in real time, while the risk core validates every fill against the 10x cap and tracks each position.

  3. 03

    Settle & prove

    Settlement completes atomically in USDC. A signed, hash-linked receipt is written to the ledger — yours forever.

Security

  • No store of funds — the platform orchestrates, you remain custodian.

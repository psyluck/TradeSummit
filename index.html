How it works

From order to record in three steps.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Bring liquidity from USDC pools and link your wallet or custody solution. Session authenticated once, at the edge.

  2. 02

    Match

    Hyperliquid's CLOB matches orders in real time, while the risk core validates every fill against the 30x cap and tracks each position.

  3. 03

    Settle & prove

    Settlement completes atomically in USDC. A signed, hash-linked receipt is written to the ledger — yours forever.
